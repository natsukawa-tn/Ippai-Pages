Log every drink

Tap +1 right on your Lock Screen. Ippai calculates the pure alcohol of every drink for you. No judgment, no notifications. Your data stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Features

🔒

+1 from the Lock Screen

After your first drink, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show "Tonight: 3 drinks · 42.0 g · 1:52" with +1 and Water buttons. Log without unlocking.

⚡️

2 seconds, from anywhere

Also log from Control Center, the Action button, Siri, and Home and Lock Screen widgets. No confirmation screens.

🍶

Asian drinks built in

Draft beer, highball, sake, shochu, strong chuhai, baijiu, huangjiu, soju, somaek, makgeolli and more — 37 drinks. Pick one and the pure alcohol is calculated.

🌙

Days start at 5 a.m.

A night out from 11 p.m. to 2 a.m. counts as one day. Choose a day start from 0:00 to 6:00.

📊

Stats with facts only

Drinks, pure alcohol, days without drinks, time of day, weekday and spending. No judging words and no external benchmarks. If you set a goal, it is simply shown next to your numbers.

💴

Home and out prices

Set separate prices for the same drink at home and when out.

💧

Water too

Log water between drinks with the Water button on the Lock Screen.

☁️

Your data is yours

No account, no servers. Records stay on your iPhone and in iCloud. CSV export and Face ID lock are available with Premium.

🌍

5 languages

Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Korean.

Please note

Ippai is for logging drinks. It is not a medical device and does not assess your health or whether you can drive. Never drink and drive. For people of legal drinking age.
