Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

The app requires no account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location.

2. Where your records are stored

Your drinks, water, custom drinks and settings are stored only on your iPhone and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud private database. There is no developer server, and the developer cannot access this data.

3. Health

Only if you turn on Write to Health in Settings, the app writes the number of drinks you log to the Health app. It never reads Health data.

4. Notifications

The app sends no notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

5. Usage statistics

To improve the app, anonymous usage signals (such as which entry point was used and how often features are used) may be counted with TelemetryDeck. They never include record content such as drink names, amounts, pure alcohol, prices or notes. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Share anonymous usage.

6. Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

7. Face ID

The Face ID lock uses iOS. Biometric data is never sent to the developer.

8. Deleting your data

You can edit or delete any entry in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. Data in iCloud can be managed in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > iCloud. Premium also lets you export everything as CSV.

9. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
