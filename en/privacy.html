Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

The app requires no account. It does not collect or send personal information (such as your name, email address or location), the content of your records, or how you use the app.

2. Where your records are stored

Your drinks, water, custom drinks and settings are stored only on your iPhone and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud private database. There is no developer server, and the developer cannot access this data.

3. Health

Only if you turn on Write to Health in Settings, the app writes the number of drinks you log to the Health app. It never reads Health data.

4. Notifications

The app sends no notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

5. Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

6. Face ID

The Face ID lock uses iOS. Biometric data is never sent to the developer.

7. Deleting your data

You can edit or delete any entry in the app. Settings > Delete All Entries removes your entries, custom drinks and drink list changes from this iPhone and from iCloud (every device using the same Apple ID). Data written to Health can be managed in the Health app. Premium also lets you export everything as CSV.

8. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
