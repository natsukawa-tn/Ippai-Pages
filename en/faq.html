FAQ

+1 doesn't appear on my Lock Screen

It appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island after you log your first drink. Check that Settings > Show on Lock Screen while drinking is on, and that Live Activities are allowed in iPhone Settings > Ippai. You can also tap Show on Lock Screen in the drinking session screen.

How do I log with the Action button?

In iPhone Settings > Action Button > Shortcut, choose "Ippai: +1" (on models with an Action button).

How do I log from Control Center?

Edit Control Center and add Ippai. Besides "+1 (same as last)", you can add buttons for a specific drink or for water.

What does +1 log?

One more of the same drink as last time. To log a different drink, tap it on the Today screen in the app. You can change any entry later.

Late-night drinks count toward the previous day

So a night out that crosses midnight stays in one day, each day starts at 5:00. Choose anything from 0:00 to 6:00 in Settings > Day starts at.

How is pure alcohol calculated?

Amount (ml) × ABV (%) × 0.8. You can show it in grams, JP drinks (10 g), US standard drinks (14 g) or UK units (8 g).

When does a drinking session end?

Automatically 3 hours after your last entry (1–8 hours in Settings). You can also end it right away with End.

I logged something by mistake

Tap an entry to edit it, or swipe left to delete it. Right after logging, you can also tap Undo at the bottom of the screen.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID, your records sync automatically.

Will the app send me notifications?

No. The app sends no notifications.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Premium stays available until the end of the current period. If Premium isn't active on a new iPhone, try Settings > Restore purchases.
